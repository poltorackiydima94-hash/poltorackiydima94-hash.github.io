Dimus Uploader

Dimus Uploader is a private tool used by its owner to upload and schedule videos on the owner's own YouTube channels (Before Electricity, Godlore, NE VIN and Race Day Clips). It sets titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and a pinned comment, and reads basic channel statistics.

The tool is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else's account.
